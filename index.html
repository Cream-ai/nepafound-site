FAQ

Is this only for contractors?

No. Any local small business that misses calls or wants to be found can use it. We're starting with home-service trades because they live on the phone.

Do I keep my phone number?

Yes. You forward your own number to us, so you can turn it off whenever you like.

Contract?

None. Month to month. Cancel by text or email, and it takes effect at the end of your billing cycle.

What runs this?

Our AI runs on a private blend of xAI and our own local servers, set up and monitored by us. Your business data lives on our servers in Luzerne County, and we only send out the minimum amount of information to perform our services. It's never sold and never used to train anyone's models. Privacy and safety are our #1 priority. Callers are told they're speaking with an automated assistant.

Is this local?

Yes! NEPA Found is owned and operated in Luzerne County. We focus on helping local small businesses here in Northeastern Pennsylvania get found and never miss a call.
