Questions owners ask

What is the NEPA Found beta?

Five local trade businesses get Back Office for $150 a month, locked for as long as they stay subscribed. In return, we ask for a short call each month for the first three months and honest feedback.

Is there a contract?

No. Month to month, cancel anytime. If you're not happy after your first month, we refund it.

Do you handle my customers' payments?

No. You get paid the way you always do. When a job's done, you mark it with the price, and we send the receipt and log it in your books.

Do I have to put every job in the books?

Your books include what you enter. Each job has a payment method option (cash, check, card, or other) so your records match how you got paid.

What happens to my data if I cancel?

It's deleted 30 days after you cancel. Billing records are kept for 7 years, as required.

Can I keep using QuickBooks?

Yes. Our books stand on their own, and you can connect QuickBooks if you or your accountant use it.
