FAQ

Is this only for contractors?

No. Any local small business that misses calls or wants to be found can use it. We're starting with home-service trades because they live on the phone.

Does it handle calls and texts?

We answer the calls. The texts go to you: you and your staff get an instant alert for each lead with the caller's name, number and reason, plus a lead summary. Alerts come by email today, and by text as soon as carrier registration clears. We don't text your callers.

What if AI visibility doesn't improve?

No improvement at the 30-day recheck = 30 more days free or a $199 refund, your choice. AI answers change, so no one can promise a specific ranking, but you won't pay for a sprint that didn't move anything. The $199 is also credited toward your first month if you upgrade to monthly.

Do I keep my phone number?

Yes. You forward your own number to us, so you can turn it off whenever you like.

Contract?

None. Month to month. Cancel by text or email, and it takes effect at the end of your billing cycle.

What runs this?

Our AI is set up and monitored by us. Your data stays on our own private systems. Never sold or shared. Privacy and safety are our #1 priority. Callers are told they're speaking with an automated assistant.

Is this local?

Yes! NEPA Found is owned and operated in Luzerne County. We focus on helping local small businesses here in Northeastern Pennsylvania get found and never miss a call.
