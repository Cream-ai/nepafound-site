Privacy Policy

Last updated October 21, 2026

NEPA Found is operated by Interregnum Ventures LLC, 91 Price Street Fl 2, Kingston, PA 18704. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Information you give us on our forms: business name, phone, email, town and trade.
  • For Call Answering customers: details of calls to your business (caller number, the message they leave, call times) so we can send you lead summaries.
  • For Back Office customers: what you put into Back Office (jobs, schedule, customer details, receipts and books) and your conversations with its assistant. See Back Office and your assistant below.
  • Billing is handled by Stripe. We don't store card numbers.

How we use it

To provide the service you signed up for: answering and summarizing calls, sending you alerts and reports, billing and support.

Sharing

Your data is stored on our own private systems. We never sell it. We share it only with providers we need to run the service (for example our phone carrier Telnyx, AI processing providers such as xAI that transcribe and summarize calls and run the Back Office backup AI described below, and Stripe for billing), or when the law requires it.

Mobile numbers and SMS opt-in data are never shared with or sold to third parties or affiliates for marketing. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

Back Office and your assistant

If you use Back Office, what you put into it and your conversations with its assistant are stored on our own servers in Kingston, PA, on encrypted storage.

Your assistant runs on those servers. In two cases it uses a backup AI service from xAI instead: when our assistant servers are unavailable or too busy, and for some harder requests our own assistant can't answer well. When that happens, that one request is sent to xAI to get the answer: your message, the records the assistant needs to answer it, and any receipt photo you asked it to read.

We use xAI's zero-data-retention service. xAI doesn't keep these requests: they're deleted once the answer is delivered (within an hour at most), with no logs, copies or backups kept, and they aren't used to train AI models. We send a coded account number, never your name, email or phone number. Updating what the assistant remembers about your business, and sorting the feedback you send us, always run on our own servers and never use the backup AI.

You can turn the backup AI off for your account in Settings, linked at the top of every Back Office page. With it off, the assistant only runs on our own servers; if they're unavailable it tells you so, and the rest of Back Office keeps working. We keep a record of when the backup AI was used for your account (the date, time and kind of use, never what you wrote) for 13 months, and you can see the count in Settings.

Text messages (SMS)

Program name: NEPA Found Call Alerts. We send call alerts, lead notices and account messages to people who opt in on the signup form at https://nepafound.com/signup. We do not send marketing texts. Text alerts start once carrier registration clears; until then alerts go by email.

  • Message frequency varies with how many calls your business gets.
  • Msg & data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP to opt out at any time (STOPALL, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END and QUIT also work). You'll get one confirmation text and no more messages. Reply START to resubscribe.
  • Reply HELP for help, or contact joel@nepafound.com.
  • Consent to receive texts is not a condition of any purchase.
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
